# Laptop Loan Program

The Computer and Information Science Department understands that having a reliable laptop is essential for completing programming assignments, laboratory activities, and software development projects. Because many CIS courses require students to use specialized software and development tools, access to a personal computer is an important part of the learning experience.

To help ensure every student has the technology they need to succeed, the department offers a limited Laptop Loan Program for students enrolled in CIS courses. Loaner laptops are available on a first-come, first-served basis for students who do not have access to a suitable computer during the semester.

## About the program

### Who can request

Students currently enrolled in computer and information science courses who do not have access to a suitable computer for coursework, programming assignments, or laboratory activities.

Need to borrow a laptop?  
Complete the [laptop loan request form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdGS7Z2kMojdCMHe6fp2ovoc5XucCdL1yuynk3wPnK7MiFjDw/viewform) to submit your request.
