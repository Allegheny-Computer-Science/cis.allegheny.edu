# CIS Department Five Year Action Plan: Driving Innovation and Elevating Student Success

As the next five years unfold, the CIS Department aims to lead CS education innovation, inclusivity, and student success.

## Keep the Curriculum Relevant

Improvements and upgrades to our curriculum have begun, aligning with the latest industry trends and emerging technologies. We are committed to delivering exceptional education that is engaging, current by industrial and academic standards, and able to assist students in productive computational careers.

## Drive Inclusivity with Systemic Changes

We continue to work toward an inclusive environment where students can thrive. Our focuses include cultivating diversity, encouraging student engagement, and creating equitable opportunities for everyone.

## Unlock New Opportunities with Microcredentials

An upgraded microcredentials program is on the horizon! These stackable credentials will empower students to showcase specialized skills in cutting-edge tech areas, allowing them to clearly show they are ready for tomorrow's challenges.

## Strengthen Recruitment and Clear Pathways

Our revamped recruitment strategies will set the stage for continued growth. Expect dynamic promotional materials, streamlined pathways, and a refreshed online presence to attract and guide future students toward industry-informed outcomes.

## Enhance Learning Through Assessment-Driven Design

We are on a journey to redesign assignments and assessment tools to further enhance learning outcomes and elevate student performance. Our goal is to create an even stronger connection between coursework and real-world success.

## Boost Career Readiness with Expanded TAB Mentorship

The Technical Advisory Board is expanding its role in career advising and mentorship, building stronger connections between students and industry professionals to prepare them for future success.

## Share Our Story by Publishing Groundbreaking Educational Research

We are reinforcing Allegheny's presence in the world of educational innovation. Our faculty are driving impactful research, shaping the future of CIS education on a national scale.
