# Driving Innovation & Elevating Student Success
Since the start of the _**In For Allegheny**_ campaign, we have been expanding our efforts to build something truly meaningful within our department. These are our goals for the next 2 years:

<div class="mdx-gold-band">
## Our 2023 Action Plan
1. Maintain a Relevant Curriculum
2. Drive Inclusivity with Systemic Changes
3. Unlock New Opportunities through Microcredentials
4. Strengthen Recruitment and Clear Pathways
5. Enhance Learning through Assessment-Driven Design
6. Boost Career Readiness with Expanded TAB Mentorship
7. Share Our Story by Publishing Groundbreaking Educational Research
</div>

***

### Keep the Curriculum Relevant
We are **refreshing and strengthening our curriculum** to reflect the technologies, practices, and skills shaping today’s computing industry and academic landscape. Our goal is an education that is not only rigorous, but **engaging, current, and responsive to where the field is headed**—giving students the knowledge and experience they need to build productive, meaningful careers in computing.

***

### Drive Inclusivity Through Systemic Change
Inclusion is more than a goal—it is an ongoing commitment. We are working to create a department where **students from every background feel welcomed, supported, and empowered to succeed**. Through intentional changes to our practices and culture, we are strengthening diversity, expanding student engagement, and creating **more equitable opportunities to participate, lead, and succeed**.

***

### Unlock New Opportunities with Microcredentials
An **upgraded microcredentials program is on the horizon**, giving students new ways to demonstrate expertise beyond the traditional course transcript. These stackable credentials will allow students to **build and showcase specialized skills in emerging areas of technology**, providing a clear signal to employers and graduate programs that they are prepared to meet the challenges of tomorrow.

***

### Strengthen Recruitment and Create Clearer Pathways
Our revamped recruitment strategy is designed to support **continued growth and a stronger connection with prospective students**. From dynamic promotional materials and a refreshed online presence to clearer academic pathways, we are creating a more cohesive experience that helps students understand **where a CIS education can take them—and how to get there**.

***

### Enhance Learning Through Assessment-Driven Design
We are redesigning assignments and assessment practices with one goal in mind: **helping students learn more effectively**. By using evidence of student learning to inform how we design coursework, we can create stronger connections between **what students learn in the classroom, what they can demonstrate, and what they will be expected to do beyond Allegheny**.

The result is a curriculum designed not simply to measure learning, but to **actively improve it**.

***

### Boost Career Readiness Through Expanded TAB Mentorship
The **Technical Advisory Board (TAB)** is expanding its role in career advising and mentorship, creating stronger connections between our students and computing professionals. By bringing industry experience closer to the classroom, we can help students **navigate career possibilities, develop professional networks, and understand the skills employers are looking for**—long before graduation.

***

### Share Our Story Through Groundbreaking Educational Research
Our commitment to innovation extends beyond what happens in the classroom. Faculty are advancing **research in computing education and educational innovation**, contributing new ideas to the broader academic community and strengthening Allegheny’s voice in conversations about how computing should be taught.

By sharing this work through **scholarship, publications, and national and international venues**, we are not simply responding to changes in CIS education—we are helping **shape what comes next**.

***
