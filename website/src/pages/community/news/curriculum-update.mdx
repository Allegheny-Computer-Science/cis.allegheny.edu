Following its annual curriculum review, the CIS department has approved a set of changes that take effect for the 2026–27 academic year. These updates reflect both student feedback and shifts in the field, particularly around AI, systems programming, and data ethics.

## New Courses

**CMPSC 312 – Foundations of Machine Learning** is a new upper-division elective covering supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and the mathematical underpinnings of modern ML pipelines. It counts toward both the CMPSC and DS majors. Prerequisite: CMPSC 201 or DS 310.

**INFM 280 – Data Ethics and Society** examines the social, legal, and ethical dimensions of data collection and algorithmic decision-making. This course fulfills a humanities distribution requirement and is open to all majors. No prerequisite.

## Revised Prerequisites

- **CMPSC 302 – Operating Systems** now requires CMPSC 201 (previously CMPSC 101). This change aligns the course with its actual content level.
- **DS 310 – Statistical Learning** has added MATH 210 as a co-requisite to ensure students have sufficient probability background.

## Advising Notes

Students with questions about how these changes affect their plans should meet with their faculty advisor before fall registration. The updated four-year plan templates on the My Path tool will reflect these changes by August 1.
