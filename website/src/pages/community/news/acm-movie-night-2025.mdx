The Allegheny ACM student chapter brought students together for a screening of *Ready Player One* at Vukovich Hall Theatre — one of a series of social and technical events the chapter ran this fall to build community across the department.

## ACM This Semester

The chapter's mission is to make technology "approachable, exciting, and boundary-free" for every student, regardless of background or experience level. Alongside the movie night, ACM ran an **SOS event** at the start of the semester to help students get their laptops set up for introductory CS courses, and is planning an **Afternoon of Code** mini-hackathon where students will build websites for campus clubs.

## Get Involved

ACM meets regularly in Alden Hall and welcomes students from all majors. Alumni interested in mentoring or judging at upcoming events can sign up via the [Alumni Volunteer Form](https://forms.gle/6AFMAQmZ7ULZrDms5).
