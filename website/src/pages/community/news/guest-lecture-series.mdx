The CIS department is hosting its third annual Summer Guest Lecture Series, bringing practitioners from industry and research institutions to campus for informal talks, Q&A sessions, and student networking. All events are free and open to the Allegheny community.

## Schedule

| Date | Speaker | Topic |
|------|---------|-------|
| June 18 | **Dr. Keely Marsh**, Google DeepMind | Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: Lessons from Production |
| July 2 | **Raj Patel**, Cloudflare | How the Edge Works: Distributed Systems at Scale |
| July 16 | **Amara Osei**, MITRE Corporation | Threat Modeling for Real-World Systems |
| July 30 | **Dr. Lena Fischer**, Carnegie Mellon | Responsible AI: What "Alignment" Actually Means |

All talks run from **4:00–5:30 PM** in **Alden Hall 101**, with an informal reception to follow.

## Format

Each session begins with a 40-minute talk, followed by open Q&A and time for students to speak one-on-one with the speaker. Attendees are encouraged to come with questions — these are not formal lectures, and the speakers have all indicated they prefer conversation to presentation.

Light refreshments will be provided. No RSVP required, though space is limited.
