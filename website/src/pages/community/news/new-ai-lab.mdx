The Allegheny College CIS department has opened a dedicated AI Research Lab in Alden Hall, providing students and faculty with access to hardware and infrastructure that was previously only available through cloud credits or external partnerships.

## What's in the Lab

The lab is equipped with four NVIDIA A100 GPU nodes, shared networked storage, and a JupyterHub instance that allows students to spin up research environments without managing local dependencies. The setup mirrors what students will encounter in graduate programs and industry research roles.

In addition to the compute hardware, the lab includes a small seminar space for group reviews, paper reading sessions, and collaborative debugging — something that was missing from the department's existing lab spaces.

## Who Can Use It

The AI Research Lab is open to any student working on a faculty-supervised research project. Priority access goes to students enrolled in **CMPSC 498 – Senior Research** and **CMPSC 499 – Senior Thesis**, but any student with a faculty sponsor can request access through the department office.

Faculty can also reserve the lab for class sessions or workshops. The lab will host its first public event — a hands-on introduction to fine-tuning language models — on May 22nd.

## Background

The lab was made possible through a combination of a National Science Foundation equipment grant and contributions from alumni donors. The department is grateful to everyone who made this possible, and particularly to the alumni who designated their gifts specifically for student research infrastructure.

Students interested in research opportunities should speak with any faculty member or visit the Opportunities page on this site.
