From May 14–22, 2025, Allegheny CIS faculty and students traveled to Pittsburgh for **PyCon US 2025**, one of the largest annual gatherings of the Python community. The department brought four presentations spanning tools, automation, and computing education.

## Presentations

**"Infomaid: An Offline AI Text-Generative Tool to Support Ethical Learning with Textual Assistance"**
*Poster — Oliver Bonham-Carter*

**"ExecExam: Streamlining Python Assessments with Automation and Personalized Feedback"**
*Poster — Greg Kapfhammer, Hemani Alaparthi '27, Pallas-Athena Cain*

**"Up and Running with GitHub, GitHub Classroom, and GitHub Actions"**
*Education Summit — Greg Kapfhammer and Hemani Alaparthi '27*

**"Automated and Configurable Programming Project Checking with Chasten"**
*Education Summit — Daniel Bekele, Greg Kapfhammer, Thao Pham*

The Education Summit presentations focused on practical tools that lower the barrier to running effective, automated computing courses — work that grows directly out of what students and faculty use every day at Allegheny.
