Allegheny's chapter of Upsilon Pi Epsilon — the international honor society for computing disciplines — had a landmark fall semester, inducting more than 20 new members across two ceremonies.

## Initiation Ceremony

On August 27, 2025, the chapter welcomed its newest class in a formal initiation ceremony. Dr. Geoffrey Draper, representing UPE's national organization, attended to mark the occasion.

## Pinning Ceremony

A follow-up pinning ceremony on October 8, 2025 brought members together to receive certificates, pins, and commemorative sweatshirts — a tradition that celebrates their commitment to academic excellence in computing.

UPE membership recognizes students who have demonstrated outstanding scholarly achievement in the computing disciplines. If you're interested in learning more about eligibility and how to get involved, visit [upeallegheny.netlify.app](https://upeallegheny.netlify.app/).
