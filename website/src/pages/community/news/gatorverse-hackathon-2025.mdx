On April 12–13, 2025, the ACM student chapter hosted the first-ever **Gatorverse Hackathon** — a 48-hour event held in Alden Hall that brought together 17 participants across ten interdisciplinary teams.

## What Happened

Teams spent the weekend building technology prototypes, supported by workshops throughout the event and guidance from alumni mentors who joined to advise and encourage participants. The interdisciplinary makeup of teams meant projects drew on perspectives from across the college — not just computing majors.
