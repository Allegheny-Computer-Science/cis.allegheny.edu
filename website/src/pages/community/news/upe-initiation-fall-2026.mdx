Allegheny's chapter of Upsilon Pi Epsilon, the international honor society for the computing disciplines, welcomed nine new members at its fall initiation ceremony on September 21, 2026.

During the ceremony, the new members took the UPE oath together and signed the chapter's membership book, officially joining a society that recognizes outstanding scholarly achievement in computing.

## New Members

- Tyler J. Biss
- Shahzaib Ellahi
- Kristian Janci
- Aidana Kuat
- Magnolia M. Myers
- Anshu Pathak
- Md Nehal Uddin Rafin
- Ashir Ul Haq
- Krish S. Yendluri

Congratulations to all nine new members! If you're interested in learning more about UPE eligibility and how to get involved, visit [upeallegheny.netlify.app](https://upeallegheny.netlify.app/).
