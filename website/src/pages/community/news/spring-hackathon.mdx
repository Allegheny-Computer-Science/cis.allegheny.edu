The CIS department's Spring Hackathon returns for its fifth year. Open to all Allegheny students regardless of major or experience level, this is one of the best opportunities of the year to build something from scratch, learn from your peers, and have a genuinely good time doing it.

## Details

- **When:** April 25–26, starting at 6:00 PM Friday
- **Where:** Alden Hall, all floors
- **Team size:** 1–4 students
- **Registration:** Opens April 14 — sign up at the link below

## Tracks

This year teams can compete in one of three tracks:

**Open** — Build anything. The only constraint is that it has to be a working demo by Sunday afternoon. Past projects have ranged from browser extensions to hardware integrations to generative art tools.

**Community Impact** — Projects must address a real problem faced by a local community, campus organization, or underserved group. Judges will weight feasibility and potential for real-world use heavily in this track.

**Beginner-Friendly** — New to programming? This track is judged differently: the panel looks for learning, creativity, and effort over technical complexity. Mentors will be available throughout the event.

## Judging and Prizes

Projects are demoed to a panel that includes faculty, a returning alum, and one industry guest. Prizes include AWS credits, hardware, and — for the Open track winner — a guaranteed summer research position in the department (pending faculty availability).

## Mentorship

Faculty and upperclass students will be available for drop-in help throughout the event. A schedule of office hours will be posted in the Alden Hall lobby on the day of the event.

Sign-up and full rules at the department office or on the department Discord.
