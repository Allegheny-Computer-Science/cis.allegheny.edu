This fall, the department's weekly **i-TEA** gathering — held Fridays from 2:00–2:45 PM in the Alden Hall Lobby — expanded into a combined alumni social featuring the "Getting to Know Your Gators & Alumni" activity and a career panel.

## Panelists

**Colton McCurdy '17** — Staff Software Engineer, Fastly *(moderator)*

**Matthew Pegula** — Staff Engineer, Shopify

**Katherine Leyonmark '22** — Senior Data Analyst, M&T Bank

**Morgan Douglas '23** — Vice President, Information Security, BNY

The conversation ranged across diverse career trajectories in technology, practical job-seeking strategies, and what the industry actually looks like day-to-day — straight from alumni who navigated it recently.

---

i-TEA runs every Friday during the semester. All students, faculty, and staff are welcome — stop by Alden Hall for conversation, connection, and refreshments.
